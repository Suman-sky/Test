About Me

Origin Story

Started on a PS2 with borrowed controllers at age 8. By 16, I was competing in regional LANs. By 21, I turned pro. Now I play to dominate and create — sharing every technique, every loss, every championship run.

The Mindset

Gaming isn't just reflex — it's pattern recognition, map theory, mental composure. I study replays like film, run drills like an athlete, and keep a game journal. Every match is data. Every death is tuition.

Off the Grid

When I'm AFK: Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, film noir, and building custom keyboards. The discipline from BJJ transferred directly to staying calm in 1v5 clutch situations. The keyboards? That's just an addiction.

Creator Mission

I stream to demystify high-level play — no gatekeeping, no ego, just raw strategy breakdowns. 340K+ subscribers. 2M monthly viewers. Building the most honest FPS education channel on the internet.
